THE WEWAL HERITAGE

The Alchemy of Steam, Reed & Patience

Wewal (cane / rattan) is an indigenous wild climbing palm that thrives in the tropical rainforest fringes of Sri Lanka.

Unlike mass-produced plastic rattan substitutes, genuine wewal retains its natural woodgrain memory. Our artisans soften mature cane stalks using steam chambers, gently bending them into flowing sculptural curves before weaving intricate honeycomb and chevron patterns.

  • ✓
    Harvested with Respect

    Only mature vines are selected, allowing the forest roots to naturally regenerate.

  • ✓
    Chemical-Free Natural Finish

    Sealed with organic beeswax and coconut-derived protective coatings.

  • ✓
    Empowering Craft Villages

    Fair living wages supporting over 80 artisan families in the central hills.
